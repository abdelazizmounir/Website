## Copy-ready video prompt

Create a **12-second horizontal 16:9 photorealistic time-lapse film** of a real urban public garden being renewed. Use the **four attached clean photographs as fixed image keyframes**, in the supplied order. They show one municipal park in Morocco from one locked-off camera position on a slightly elevated walkway. The opening frame must match keyframe 01; the mid-work state must match keyframe 02; the empty finished garden must match keyframe 03; and the closing public-use frame must match keyframe 04. Preserve the cream buildings and boundary wall in the background, mature olive and palm trees, curved central pedestrian path, benches, black lampposts, horizon, lens and camera framing throughout. Animate the work BETWEEN the supplied images; do not invent another park or camera angle.

**0.0–1.5 seconds, before:** Show the tired public garden: patchy grass, weeds, dry planting beds and worn path edges. Hold the composition so viewers understand the original condition.

**1.5–5.0 seconds, preparation:** In realistic time-lapse, landscape workers in plain unbranded workwear remove weeds and debris, prune existing trees, level soil, install irrigation lines and repair the curved path edges. Workers, wheelbarrows and tools move quickly, while the buildings, trees, benches, lampposts and camera remain stable. Construction steps happen in a believable order.

**5.0–7.5 seconds, planting:** Workers lay healthy turf and plant shrubs, seasonal flowers and young trees. Add mulch and finish the beds. Show the work being done by people and tools; plants do not grow magically or pop into place.

**7.5–10.0 seconds, after:** The same park is finished, clean and peaceful. The curved path is clear and accessible, lawns are even, flower beds are maintained, and benches and lamps are clean. Hold the completed space briefly without workers or visitors to make the before-and-after transformation clear.

**10.0–12.0 seconds, public use:** A few residents enter naturally, walk on the path and sit on benches. Include a family walking together and one older visitor resting. Keep the garden visible and uncrowded. End on the welcoming completed public space.

Visual style: authentic documentary footage, natural Moroccan daylight, realistic shadows, restrained color grading, plausible vegetation, and steady framing. Convey the speed of work through time-lapse motion while keeping the location geographically consistent. The story should work with sound off. If sound is included, use subtle city ambience, gardening sounds and a light uplifting instrumental bed.

**The output is one full-frame view at every moment: never show a collage, split screen, panel border, scene number, timer, caption or title card. Do not include any company name, logo, initials, wordmark, signs, text on workwear or equipment, or branded end screen.** Avoid changing buildings, moving the path or mature trees, duplicate workers, instant plant growth, plastic-looking greenery, dramatic weather changes, CGI appearance or a different camera angle between before and after. If a requested motion conflicts with a supplied keyframe, preserve the keyframe.

## Generation setup

If the model allows timed keyframes, assign the four stills at **0, 5, 9 and 12 seconds**. Set reference fidelity high, stylization low, camera motion off and text generation off. If the model accepts only start and end frames, generate three shorter clips (before → work, work → after, after → public use) and join them in editing. This gives the model a concrete visual target at each stage and avoids feeding it the numbered storyboard grid.
