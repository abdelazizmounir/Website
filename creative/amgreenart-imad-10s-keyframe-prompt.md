## Copy-ready master prompt

Create a **10-second, 9:16, photorealistic mobile brand film** for the collaboration between **I.MAD** and **AM GREEN ART**. Use the attached storyboard for the sequence, and use the two standalone aerial images as **fixed visual keyframes**. They show **one and the same physical villa and garden** from two camera angles. Preserve their design, spelling, proportions, positions, materials, light direction and landscaping.

**0.0–1.4 seconds — Doors and unfinished garden.** Start with the carved opaque cedar villa doors fully closed, matching storyboard panel 01. A fixed eye-level camera faces them from inside. The doors open onto the messy version of this garden: uneven grass, overgrown beds and an inactive fountain. Keep the villa architecture and door geometry fixed.

**1.4–5.1 seconds — Realistic landscaping time-lapse.** Two or three workers level soil, install irrigation, lay healthy grass, prune shrubs and bonsai, shape the planting beds, clean the curved stone paths and start the fountain. Show plausible work, with the garden changing but the building staying fixed. End on the attractive completed garden from the doorway, as in storyboard panel 04. The garden lettering is hidden by this low viewing angle.

**5.1–6.9 seconds — Drone rise and exact overhead reveal.** Transition to a drone rising over that same garden. Arrive at the **exact composition of attached keyframe 06** and hold long enough to read it. The villa occupies the top of the vertical frame. Curved pale paths, palms and flower beds surround an off-center circular fountain. On the large central lawn, physical landscaping reads **I. × AM GREEN** from left to right: a tall bright clipped-hedge **I** with a separate round dot on the **left**, a small pale stone **×** in the **center**, and dark clipped-hedge **AM** above **GREEN** on the **right**. Match keyframe 06's exact letter shapes, spacing and placement. These are built garden elements, never floating graphic text.

**6.9–8.2 seconds — Exact oblique view and orbit.** Move only the drone camera from the overhead position into the **exact camera angle and composition of attached keyframe 07**. Pass through that view clearly. Give the sense of a fast, smooth 360-degree drone orbit around the finished villa garden using a restrained speed ramp. The villa, fountain, paths, trees, hedge letters, shadows and surrounding terrain remain the same fixed physical objects. Never rotate, rebuild or morph the ground. When motion and accuracy compete, preserve the two aerial keyframes and their garden layout.

**8.2–10.0 seconds — Return and loop.** Descend or use a natural matched cut to the original eye-level doorway view. The same cedar doors close. End on a frame identical to the opening frame in framing, lens, door position, wood pattern, exposure and color, giving a seamless loop.

Style: real drone and camera footage at a Moroccan villa near Fès–Meknès; warm joyful morning light; natural skin, plants, water, materials and shadows; restrained color grade; documentary realism. The transformation is time-lapsed, but every action is physically plausible. The film must work without sound.

**Strict visual constraints:** do not change the overhead garden design between keyframes 06 and 07; do not reverse the brand order; do not misspell, duplicate, omit or relocate any part of **I. × AM GREEN**; do not add “ART” to the lawn; do not show lettering before the aerial reveal; do not introduce another fountain, villa or path; do not make the landscape spin, stretch or warp; do not use CGI-looking plants, floating logos, glass doors or a visible jump at the loop seam.

## Accuracy note

Video models can still alter small hedge letters during camera movement. If the exact mark changes, keep the original keyframe 06 and 07 imagery as the aerial plates and animate the camera transition or use tracked compositing in editing. Prompt wording alone cannot guarantee pixel-exact lettering.
