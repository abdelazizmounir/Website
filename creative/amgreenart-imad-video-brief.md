## Master prompt for a video generation model

Create a 24-second photorealistic cinematic brand film, 16:9 landscape, designed as a seamless website loop and with a center-safe composition for a 9:16 social crop. Use the supplied eight-frame storyboard as the visual continuity reference. Use the supplied I.MAD I-and-dot monogram as the shape reference only. The film should feel warm, joyful, premium and believable, like high-quality architectural photography in a Moroccan villa near Fès–Meknès.

One locked camera at eye level inside the villa foyer faces the same pair of solid, opaque carved cedar double doors for the entire video. No camera movement, reframing, lens change, or jump cut. The cream stone walls, floor, door handles, garden path, fountain and large topiary stay in exactly the same positions. Morning sunlight has one consistent direction.

Start with both opaque doors fully closed. They open outward to reveal an untidy garden: rough grass, overgrown planting beds, an inactive fountain and one large shapeless green topiary near the center. Two to three landscape workers enter and perform a physically plausible accelerated transformation. Show them checking the terrain, installing irrigation, preparing soil, laying or mowing grass, planting and tending a bonsai, trimming hedges, restoring the fountain and carefully pruning the original topiary. Use smooth time-lapse motion on the workers and work only; architecture and camera remain still. Avoid cloning workers or making plants appear from nowhere.

As the workers finish, the grass looks healthy, the garden is balanced and tidy, the bonsai is refined, and the fountain begins to flow. The workers leave the frame. The same original topiary is now trimmed into a clear I.MAD monogram: one tall simple upright foliage rectangle forming the capital I, with one separate, unmistakable round foliage ball to its lower right forming the dot. Both forms should feel rooted in the same planted topiary composition. Hold this reveal long enough to recognize the I and dot. A gentle golden highlight on the upright and soft bright highlight on the dot may echo the supplied yellow-and-white brand mark while the living foliage remains naturally green.

For the social collaboration version, leave a clean area for authentic logo overlays during this reveal. The official AM GREEN ART and I.MAD logos and all words will be placed in postproduction, not generated in the scene. Close the same doors. The final fully closed-door frame must match the first frame exactly in camera, geometry, door position, indoor lighting and color so the loop has no visible seam.

Visual style: realistic foliage and water, natural skin and clothing, subtle motion blur from the time lapse, restrained color grading, optimistic morning energy, no fantasy growth effects. Silent viewing must tell the full story. Optional sound design for the social version: doors opening, shears, water, a gentle rhythmic rise during the work, and a calm resolution at the I-and-dot reveal.

Avoid: glass or transparent doors, extra letters, invented logos, generated text, changing villa architecture, shifting fountain or path, jump cuts, drone views, cartoon or glossy CGI, floating tools, impossible plant morphs, and a final frame different from the first.
