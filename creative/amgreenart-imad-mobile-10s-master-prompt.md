## Master prompt for an omni video model

Generate a **10-second, 9:16 vertical, photorealistic mobile brand film** for a landscaping collaboration between AM GREEN ART and I.MAD. Use the attached eight-frame storyboard as the visual and architectural reference. It must look like real footage from a Moroccan villa near Fès–Meknès: real plants, real workers, natural skin and clothing, believable construction steps, slight environmental imperfections and warm joyful morning light. Avoid the glossy CGI or synthetic ad look.

**0.0–1.4 seconds:** A fixed eye-level camera stands just inside a villa entrance, centered on two solid opaque cedar doors. Start with the doors fully closed. They open outward to reveal a genuinely untidy garden: rough lawn, overgrown planting beds and an inactive fountain. The same doors, cream plaster, floor, pathway and fountain must remain consistent throughout. No brand letters are visible from this low camera angle.

**1.4–5.1 seconds:** Time-lapse the work by two or three AM GREEN ART landscapers. Show practical, physically plausible actions: surveying the plot, installing irrigation, leveling soil, laying or mowing healthy gazon, planting, pruning a bonsai and existing shrubs, and starting the fountain. Keep the villa and hardscape stable while only workers and garden work accelerate. End on a complete, naturally attractive green space seen through the still-open doors. It feels cared for, not unnaturally perfect. Do not reveal any wordmark at ground level.

**5.1–8.2 seconds:** Use a believable matched cut at the doorway or a brief foliage wipe to a drone camera outside. The drone rises above the same garden. At a true top-down angle, hold long enough to discover that the whole planned garden composition reads **“I. × AM GREEN” from left to right**. Make the I. a large, distinct clipped hedge on the left with its separate circular dot; form a small × from crossing pale stone paths in the center; arrange clipped hedge letters AM above GREEN on the right so the full mark stays legible in a vertical mobile frame. Use an organic garden plan with curved paths and an off-center fountain, as shown in the new storyboard. The inscription has existed in the finished landscaping, but perspective concealed it from the doorway. Keep the lawn, bonsai, planting beds and fountain recognizable around the mark. Then show a time-compressed 360-degree drone orbit around the fixed villa and garden: the drone moves; the house and ground do not rotate or warp. Keep the garden near the center of the 9:16 frame.

**8.2–10.0 seconds:** The camera descends and returns to the exact original fixed doorway viewpoint through a natural matched cut. The same cedar doors close. End on a frame identical to the opening frame in door position, handles, wood pattern, floor, lens, framing, exposure and color so the clip loops without a visual jump.

Camera and grade: natural photographic lens, realistic depth and shadows, high-detail vegetation, warm sunlight, restrained contrast, documentary realism, subtle film grain. The fast work and drone orbit may be sped up; avoid impossible physics or magical plant growth. The story must remain clear with sound off. If sound is generated, use a door creak, natural tool sounds, water and a short joyful rhythmic rise that resolves before the doors close.

**Avoid:** horizontal framing, glass doors, fake or misspelled letters, text visible before the top-down reveal, floating graphic logos, changing villa geometry, duplicate workers, plastic plants, a miniature/toy appearance, a spinning world, abrupt day/night shifts and a mismatched loop seam.

## Practical finishing note

The overhead garden lettering is the hero visual. If the video model distorts the exact characters, track the real aerial garden plate and finish **I. × AM GREEN** in the specified left-to-right layout with a precise composited overlay using the supplied I.MAD reference mark (`imad-i-dot-reference.png`) rather than publishing a misspelling. Remove all overlay elements before the doors close. Use frame 01 as the exact last frame in editing if needed for a seamless loop.
